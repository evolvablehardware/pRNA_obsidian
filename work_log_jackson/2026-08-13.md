## Log
- Finished genome extraction from last experiment, results seem alright. There is however a heavy dependence on other genomes being on the same circuit from some, but not all, genome+pad arrangements. Based on previous results, I know that this can be in part from the temperature increase from having greater circuit usage. I am confident that as far as the documented features are concerned, the genomes cannot interact with each other. However, there is a good bit of undefined behavior already going on, especially since the luts are not being clocked (at least they are not configured to be...). I suspect that the span nets carry some amount of signal even when they are not configured. At some point we could try evolution without them, but I am skeptical whether this would leave large enough of a search space to do anything significant with - we actually need some amount of undefined behavior in order to generate signals in the first place as there are no clocked luts.
- I'd like to explore enabling the lut clocks at some point. By relying on less undefined behavior, we won't have to fight to keep genomes stable across multiple fpgas/circuit locations if we want to do more evaluations at once. The main concern is knowing how many gates need to be present on the chip in order for it to be faster than simulation. 
	~100m gates/s with verilator, need 2.5k with 40khz for same throughput. That's about same amount of logic cells that are currently being used, which carry much more power as they can be used as muxs
